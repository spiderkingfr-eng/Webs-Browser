Privacy extras

Warn me about shops that might be fakeAs a shop opens, Webs looks at its discounts, the pages it links to, how it takes money, its address and how old the website is (only the website's name goes to the public registry, through Web AI's server). A bar on the page says why.
Clean up a site when I close itIts cookies and stored data go when you close its last tab, and you're signed out of it. Pick the sites from the panel left of the address.
ContainersKeep a site in a window of its own, with its own cookies and logins (Shopping, Work, Banking…): Menu → Privacy tools → Containers.
What sites see about youYour internet address and roughly where you are, your device and the fingerprint a site can build: Menu → Privacy tools.

Voice control

Listen for “Hey Webs”Say “Hey Webs”, then a command: over a hundred, like “open YouTube”, “scroll down”, “pause”, “set a timer for 10 minutes” or “what's happening near me”. Only the words after the wake phrase are used, nothing is recorded or kept, and never in private windows. The 🎙️ button in the toolbar (or Alt+Shift+M) turns it on and off, and lists every command.
Answer out loudWebs says what it did, and answers questions.
Use the offline engineIf speech recognition doesn't work on this PC: Vosk, a 40 MB download once, after which nothing you say leaves the PC.
