Shield

Block ads and trackers Stops ad servers, trackers and malware sites before they load, and hides what is left. Turn it off for one site from the shield button in the toolbar.
Block popups and pop-unders Windows a page opens without you clicking, and known ad popups even when you did.
Skip YouTube ads Removes ads from the player and the feed. Anything that still gets through is muted and skipped.

Filter lists

My filters

One rule per line, in uBlock Origin / EasyList syntax. Elements you remove with Block an element are added here. Examples: ||ads.example.com^ blocks a server, example.com##.promo hides something on one site, @@||example.com^ never blocks a site.

About

Webs Browser — a single Windows executable with no bundled runtime. Pages are rendered by the Microsoft Edge WebView2 engine that is already part of Windows, so it stays current with Edge's security updates. Shield uses the same filter lists as uBlock Origin, fetched straight from their publishers and refreshed every few days. The free VPN runs the official Tor client from torproject.org, checked against its published checksum.
